About

The short version

I'm a software engineer in Aotearoa New Zealand with a first-class honours degree and a soft spot for clean, efficient code. I've been hooked on programming since I was 14. Back then it was Scratch; now it's search platforms in the cloud.

People tend to describe me as “onto it”. I like setting a goal, owning it end to end and getting it over the line, ideally with a few laughs along the way. I can explain tech without the jargon, and I'm usually the one who remembers why that decision was made.
